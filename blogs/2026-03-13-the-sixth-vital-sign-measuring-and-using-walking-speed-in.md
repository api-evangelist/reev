Did you know that walking speed can predict hospitalization, falls, and even mortality? When most people think of vital signs, they picture heart rate, blood pressure, respiratory rate, and temperature. These are the objective measurements that offer a quick snapshot of a patient’s health and help guide clinical decisions.
